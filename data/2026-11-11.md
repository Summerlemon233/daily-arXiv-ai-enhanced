Abstract: Skipping zero activations saves multiplications in CNN accelerators, but it complicates accumulation: the products in a compressed input window belong to different output columns and must be merged. In our earlier engine, this merging was done by read-modify-write (RMW) of a row buffer, which made sparse inputs slower than dense ones even at 87% sparsity.
